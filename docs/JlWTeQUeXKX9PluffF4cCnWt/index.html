How the creative hub is built
  1. Parsing. Ad names follow Product - Format - [Partnership] - [Creator] - Angle - Description - Type - Landing - Date. The parser anchors from both ends because the middle is variable length. Creator is whatever sits immediately before Partnership, which is why lowercase handles are read correctly.
  2. Offers are excluded, Rs 22,881 this week. PriceSlash, FreebieOffer, AOV999 and similar are promo levers, not creative angles.
  3. Best vs worst is split on the product's own average. An entry is a leader only if it is at or above this product's blended ROAS, a laggard only if it is below. That makes it impossible for one entry to sit in both lists, and it stops a healthy entry being flagged Worst just for being the lowest of a strong set. Up to two a side.
  4. Per-ad floor of Rs 5,000. An entry is ranked on its aggregate spend, but inside a row only ads that individually clear Rs 5,000 get a thumbnail and a per-ad ROAS. Ads with a few hundred rupees behind them carry a meaningless ROAS, so they are collapsed into a single line instead of polluting the view.
  5. Brand line of 1.3x is the absolute bar, on the Meta last-click basis. It exists because a product's own average can be poor, and the best entry inside a poor product is still poor. A product whose blended ROAS is below 1.3x is badged below brand line in its header. Scale means an entry beats its product average by 20% or more AND clears 1.3x. A leader that sits below 1.3x is tagged Below 1.3x line instead of praised. Cut means at or below 0.75x its product average, or under 0.78x absolute. No chip means the gap is not decisive.
  6. Creative type is a thin dimension. Almost everything is Video or Static, and in a single week only a handful of products carry enough of both to compare. Read it as a sanity check, not a strategy input.
  7. Thumbnails come from Meta's CDN and the links open the ad in Ads Manager. The image URLs are signed and expire in one to four days, so they are refreshed on every daily run. If a run is skipped for more than a day some images may show blank while the numbers stay correct.

How to read this
  1. The pace marker is the point. The dark tick on every bar sits at 86%, where the week has actually got to. A fill short of the tick is behind pace, a fill past it is ahead. Judge against the tick, never against a full bar.
  2. Product MER, not ROAS. Numerator is all channel Shopify net sales for the product including organic and quick commerce. Denominator is only the spend we could attribute to that product. Products with organic pull read high, products carrying prospecting spend read low. This will not tie to Meta reported ROAS.
  3. Total spend is (Meta + Google) x 1.18. The sheet states targets and target ROAS on GST inclusive spend, so MER and its target are directly comparable.
  4. Targets are the stated weekly figures from the Internal Weekly Checkpoint tab, not a pro rata of the month. Weeks with targets today: W1, W2, W3.
  5. Google product coverage is thin. Only five campaigns carry a product token. 86% of Google spend is brand, shopping or generic Pmax and sits in Unassigned, so per product Google spend is understated throughout.
  6. Smooth Operator shows zero spend by design. The short form SO was excluded from the product logic as ambiguous and no ad spells the name out, so its ads sit in Unassigned. Restoring the token would fix that row.
  7. Mapping matches the product word or its short form on token boundaries. GSIS resolves before SIS, Pouch maps to Don't Sweat It, Detox to Detox on Demand, H&D to High & Dry. First match wins, nothing is double counted, FIAF refill and jar combos stay inside FIAF Family.
  8. Creative count is not in this version. Weekly creative targets exist in the sheet but the Meta Creatives Update tab cannot be read end to end yet.
  9. September context. Month target is Rs 1.60 Cr spend incl GST against Rs 2.00 Cr revenue at 1.25.
