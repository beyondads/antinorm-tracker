How the creative hub is built
  1. Parsing. Ad names follow Product - Format - [Partnership] - [Creator] - Angle - Description - Type - Landing - Date. The parser anchors from both ends because the middle is variable length: date, landing page and creative type come off the right, product and format off the left, and the first token left in the middle is the angle.
  2. Offers are excluded. Ads led by PriceSlash, FreebieOffer, AOV999 and similar are left out entirely, Rs 26,869 this week. A discount is a promo lever, not a creative angle, and mixing them would flatter or punish the angle underneath.
  3. Legacy names are mapped. Free text names from before the convention, such as "Rid of Cigarettes Smell", are folded into their standard angle, in that case Cigarette.
  4. Verdicts. Scale needs an index of 1.25 or better AND an absolute ROAS at or above the product's target. Cut is an index at or below 0.70, or a ROAS under half the target. "Best of a weak set" means it beats its peers but still misses the target in absolute terms, which is a brief-more signal, not a scale signal.
  5. Attribution. Meta's own reported purchase value at ad level. It runs on a different basis from the Shopify revenue used above, so the two will not tie. Use this section to compare creatives against each other, and the section above to judge the business.

How to read this
  1. The pace marker is the point. The dark tick on every bar sits at 71%, where the week has actually got to. A fill short of the tick is behind pace, a fill past it is ahead. Judge against the tick, never against a full bar.
  2. Product MER, not ROAS. Numerator is all channel Shopify net sales for the product including organic and quick commerce. Denominator is only the spend we could attribute to that product. Products with organic pull read high, products carrying prospecting spend read low. This will not tie to Meta reported ROAS.
  3. Total spend is (Meta + Google) x 1.18. The sheet states targets and target ROAS on GST inclusive spend, so MER and its target are directly comparable.
  4. Targets are the stated weekly figures from the Internal Weekly Checkpoint tab, not a pro rata of the month. Weeks with targets today: W1, W2.
  5. Google product coverage is thin. Only five campaigns carry a product token. 83% of Google spend is brand, shopping or generic Pmax and sits in Unassigned, so per product Google spend is understated throughout.
  6. Smooth Operator shows zero spend by design. The short form SO was excluded from the product logic as ambiguous and no ad spells the name out, so its ads sit in Unassigned. Restoring the token would fix that row.
  7. Mapping matches the product word or its short form on token boundaries. GSIS resolves before SIS, Pouch maps to Don't Sweat It, Detox to Detox on Demand, H&D to High & Dry. First match wins, nothing is double counted, FIAF refill and jar combos stay inside FIAF Family.
  8. Creative count is not in this version. Weekly creative targets exist in the sheet but the Meta Creatives Update tab cannot be read end to end yet.
  9. September context. Month target is Rs 1.60 Cr spend incl GST against Rs 2.00 Cr revenue at 1.25.
